What is Loop Engineering

"I don’t write prompts for Claude anymore. I write loops, and the loops do the work." -- Boris Cherny, Head of Claude Code, Anthropic

Loop Engineering is the approach where automated loops prompt the agents, not the human. Instead of typing prompts one by one, you build systems that launch, monitor, and iterate on AI agents in a continuous loop.

This course explores the approach without the hype. We cover the real advantages, hidden costs, technical implementation, and practical examples with prompts ready to copy. An honest take: loops are powerful, but they're not a silver bullet.
